you're a hopeful romantic in the 25th century

The tale of two entites in a world reborn after catastrophe

A tent hosts two entites tale in a world reborn after catastrophy. Viewer is invited to join the couple intimacy, between the rubble of a contemporary past and new hybrid spaces, where nature and artifice blend in symbiosis, canceling space and time conventions. To allow themselves a moment of imperfection and think about how, maybe, apocalypse wouldn't be that bad.
