Terre d'Argento

a geriatric internet role-playing game

Terre d'Argento is a role-playing game that explores the expressive power of the geriatric internet, a realm of the web created and inhabited by people beyond the middle age, giving life to an aesthetically intense kingdom: hearts, glitter, good morning images, and other visual tricks.

Thanks to 7 dedicated TikTok profiles, players will dive in this surreal world, embarking on epic adventures and using the videos proposed by their feed to steer the narrative in their favor.
