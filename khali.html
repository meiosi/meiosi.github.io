Gli Inferni di Rub' Al Khali

Unravel the misteries behind NEOM The Line

Gli inferni di Rub' al-Khali investigates the reality behind The Line: a city 170 km long and 200 meters wide that will be developed in the middle of the Saudi Arabian desert. Through a thorough investigation, on the edge of conspiracy theories, the work sheds light on the darker sides of the NEOM project, marked by human rights violations, unfulfilled promises, and utopian dreams that pretend to be solarpunk but closely resemble the grim reality of 1970s dark sci-fi movements.
