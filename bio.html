Alessandro Iosia is an Italian based multidisciplinary artist and creative tecnologist focused on new media art. His works revolve around Internet cultures and communities, and how humanity grow and interact in these virtual spaces.

As a big fan of tabeltop RPGs he explores new realities in his projects, proposing ludic and partecipatory experiences focused on moments of pure and sincere humanity. He believes that art should be a communital experience of collaborative storytelling.
