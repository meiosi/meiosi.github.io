The Day I've Been Replaced

a dizzying descent into the uncanny valley, prompting us to question our fear of something so similar to a human, yet not human at all.

In 1970, professor Mashito Mori conceptualized the Uncanny Valley Theory: the more an anthropomorphic entity resembles a human being, the more unsettling it appears to us.
The Day I've Been Replaced is a vr experience where the viewer dive into Porfessor Mori's theory, descending the layers of a literal valley they will encounter multiple iteratrions of a few 3D charachters, each more realistic than the last. Through the interaction with the entities of the valley the viewer will be prompted to question their fear of something so similar to a human, yet not human at all.
