452b soil sample visions

listen to the song of the soil. join our mycelium.

Unusual movements emerge from the void, sensory residuals that drive bodies in a place where everything is unclear. The cosmic humus hold forsaken voices speaking for the ground, their presence is calming, yet boldly determined, it melts time and opens the space, like an invitation to become new spores of the galactic mycelium. Memories disrupt, the rot intertwines with them to regenerate their soul as visions of symbiotic futures, that host records of distant realms.
452b soil sample visions is an immersive and progressive audiovisual performance, where sounds and images co-generate, building a perceptual space between performers and the public.
